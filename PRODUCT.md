# LeadGenAI (leadgenai.io)

## What it is
A B2B lead-system builder (not a lead seller): a done-for-you service for niche B2B companies whose markets are too small, too specialised or too slow-moving for Meta Ads and Google Ads. LeadGenAI builds and runs the system for the client: maps every company in the market, watches public buying signals (permits, hiring, filings, funding, leadership and vendor changes), finds the buying committee, runs researched multichannel outreach (email, LinkedIn, phone, direct mail), and reports on qualified meetings and pipeline.

## Who it is for
Owners, founders and sales leaders of B2B firms with a total market of hundreds to a few thousand companies and deal values of roughly $10k and up. Kept broad across verticals (industrial, engineering, trades, compliance, professional services, specialty distribution, vertical SaaS). They have usually tried paid ads and got expensive or junk leads; growth still runs on referrals and the founder's network.

## The one action
Request a free pipeline audit (market size, signals worth tracking, a sample of in-market accounts, delivered within 48 hours).

## Tone
Sharp and technical. An operator talking to operators. Specific numbers, plain statements, no hype.
