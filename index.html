The problem

Paid social and search were built for mass markets. Yours isn't one.

  • Your audience is too small to targetAd platforms need large audiences to optimize. A few thousand plant managers or compliance officers never exits the "learning phase."
  • Nobody searches for what you sellBuyers of specialized services don't Google "industrial wastewater consultant near me." They ask peers, or they wait for a trigger event.
  • Clicks cost more than the lead is worthB2B keywords run $40–$150 a click, and most go to students, competitors and tire-kickers.
  • Long cycles, many stakeholdersOne ad click doesn't move a six-person buying committee through a nine-month decision.
In niche B2B, the win isn't more traffic. It's knowing which 50 accounts are ready this quarter and reaching them first. The LeadGenAI approach
