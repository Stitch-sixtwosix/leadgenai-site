Why ads stall

Ads are built for big markets. Yours isn’t one.

Meta and Google work when millions of people might buy. When your whole market fits in a spreadsheet, you pay to show ads to the wrong people and wait for an algorithm that never gets enough data.

$40–150

per clickTypical cost of specialist B2B search terms, before anyone fills in a form.

~50

conversions a weekRoughly what ad algorithms need to learn. A niche market rarely produces that many.

6–10

people in a buying groupAn ad reaches one of them, if any. Deals need all of them.

You don’t need more reach. You need to know which companies are ready to buy, who decides, and what to say to them.

FAQ

Questions

Anything else, email contact@leadgenai.io.

Do you just sell leads?

No. We build the system that produces them: the market map, signal tracking, outreach infrastructure and copy, landing pages, ads where they help, and reporting connected to your CRM. Then we run it with you, and your team takes the calls.

Where does the data come from?

Public sources and licensed databases: company registries, job boards, permit and procurement records, funding announcements, websites and professional profiles. Every contact is verified before it’s used.

Is this just cold email?

No. Email is one channel. We combine it with LinkedIn, phone, direct mail and targeted ads, and we only reach out when there’s a reason to: a signal that makes the message relevant.

Do you still run Meta and Google Ads?

Yes, when they help. We point them at the accounts we’ve already mapped, as retargeting and account-based campaigns, rather than buying broad clicks in a market too small for them.

How soon will we see meetings?

Outreach is live by the end of week four. How quickly meetings follow depends on your market and offer; the free audit gives you a realistic estimate before you commit.

Will it hurt our email domain?

No. Outreach is sent from separate, warmed domains with strict volume limits, so your main domain and its deliverability are untouched.

What does the free audit cost?

Nothing, and there’s no obligation. You get the audit within 48 hours either way.
