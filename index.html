Why ads stall

Ads are built for big markets. Yours isn’t one.

Meta and Google work when millions of people might buy. When your whole market fits in a spreadsheet, you pay to show ads to the wrong people. And sophisticated buyers delete generic cold email and ignore agency pitches.

$40–150

per clickTypical cost of specialist B2B search terms, before anyone fills in a form.

~50

conversions a weekRoughly what ad algorithms need to learn. A niche market rarely produces that many.

6–10

people in a buying groupAn ad reaches one of them, if any. Deals need all of them.

You don’t need more reach. You need to know which companies are ready to buy, who decides, and what to say when you reach out directly.

FAQ

Questions

Anything else, email contact@leadgenai.io.

Do you just sell leads?

No. We set up the system that produces them: the market map, signal tracking, buying-group research, outreach infrastructure and messaging, and reporting connected to your CRM. Your team uses it to reach out directly.

Where does the data come from?

Public sources and licensed databases: company registries, job boards, permit and procurement records, funding announcements, websites and professional profiles. Every contact is verified before it’s used.

Is this just cold email?

No. Sophisticated buyers ignore generic cold email. The system tells your team who to contact and why now, and outreach runs across email, LinkedIn, phone and direct mail only when a signal makes the message relevant.

Do you run ads?

No. We’re not an ad agency. Our clients sell into markets that don’t respond to Meta or Google Ads, so we build the system for reaching buyers directly instead.

How soon will we see meetings?

The system is live by the end of week four. How quickly meetings follow depends on your market and offer; the free audit gives you a realistic estimate before you commit.

Will it hurt our email domain?

No. Outreach is sent from separate, warmed domains with strict volume limits, so your main domain and its deliverability are untouched.

What does the free audit cost?

Nothing, and there’s no obligation. You get the audit within 48 hours either way.
