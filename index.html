    Why ads stall

    Meta and Google Ads learn from volume. Your market doesn’t have any.

    An ad set needs about 50 conversions a week before the platform stops guessing. When everyone who could ever buy from you fits in a filing cabinet, it never gets there.

    Your buyers don’t search for what you sell either. A plant manager doesn’t type “wastewater compliance consultant” into Google. They wait until something changes: a permit, a new hire, an audit, an expansion.

    That change is public, and it happens weeks before they call anyone. We watch for it across your whole market, then reach the right person first.

    The first 30 days

    1. We study your wins

      Days 1–5. A call with you and your CRM export. What closed, how big, and what changed at the customer just before.

    2. We list every company

      Days 5–10. Your full market, deduplicated and ranked. Usually a few hundred to a few thousand accounts.

    3. We wire up the signals

      Days 8–14. The permits, roles and filings that preceded your past wins, watched across every account.

    4. You approve the words

      Days 12–18. Domains warmed, sequences written per trigger and role. Nothing goes out without your sign-off.

    5. The first accounts hear from you

      Days 18–30. Outreach starts with the accounts showing signals now. Replies route to you or to us.

    6. Then every week

      Ongoing. Meetings land on your calendar. We report cost per meeting and tune the signals against who replied.

    Questions

    How is this different from buying a list? A list is a snapshot of whoever a vendor tracks. We build your whole market from primary sources, rank it every week by fit and timing, and run the outreach. You get meetings, not a spreadsheet.

    Do you run ads at all? No. If paid search already works for you profitably, keep it. This is for the markets where it doesn’t.

    How soon do meetings start? Outreach usually starts in the third or fourth week. How many meetings follow depends on your market size and offer, which the free audit estimates.

    Who writes the messages? We do, for each trigger and role. You approve every sequence before anything is sent.

    Is cold outreach allowed? Business-to-business email is legal in the US under CAN-SPAM when it is honest and offers an opt-out. For the UK and EU we follow legitimate-interest rules and handle opt-outs promptly.

    Will this hurt our email domain? We send from separate, warmed domains with low daily volumes, so your main domain is never at risk.

    What do you need from us? One kickoff call, a CRM export of past wins, approval of the messages, and someone to take the meetings.

    Which CRMs do you work with? HubSpot, Salesforce and Pipedrive directly. Anything else by CSV or Zapier.

    What does it cost? A monthly fee, quoted after the audit because it depends on market size and channels. The audit itself is free.

    What’s in the free audit? Your market size, the signals worth watching, a sample of accounts showing them now, and an honest view on whether this will work for you.
